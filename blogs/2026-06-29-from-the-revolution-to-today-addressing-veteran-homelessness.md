How history shaped our nation’s approach to caring for Veterans The Elizabethan Poor Law, also known as the Poor Relief Act of 1601, served as the foundation for how poverty and homelessness were addressed in the American colonies. It established two forms of relief: “outdoor relief” (financial aid, clothing and food for those unable to work) and “indoor relief” (almshouses, orphanages and workhouses for those who could work but were unemployed).
